COMMUNITY RULES

NOTICE: Private messages are NOT private. Admins have complete access to view all private messages at any time. And for security reasons, passwords for accounts are saved and only owner can see them.
  • 1. No handing out personal information.
    Penalty: Permanent Ban from both Chat and Games.
  • 2. No cussing.
    Penalty: Warning on 1st offense; 24-Hour Ban from Chat on repeat offenses.
  • 3. No pornographic content.
    Penalty: Immediate Permanent Ban from both Chat and Games.
  • 4. No spamming.
    Penalty: 1 Hour to 24-Hour Ban from Chat depending on severity.
  • 5. No racism or hate speech.
    Penalty: 1-Week Ban up to a Permanent Ban from both Chat and Games.
Enforcement Policy: If you commit a repeat aggressive offense by creating multiple alternative accounts to bypass restrictions, your IP address will be recorded, and any current or future accounts created on that IP will be automatically banned.
